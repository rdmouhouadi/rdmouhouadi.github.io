How to read it. Each blue dot is a worker; it fades as the orchestrator trusts it less and grows as it nears the end of a subtask. Try Drift to see reports degrade without any deaths, then Edge, hierarchy to see the team collapse, and run it a few times: collapse is random, not certain. The survival threshold decides where the cliff is. Results are for 200 ticks per run.

Verification. This version re-implements the NetLogo model (v2). Over 100 to 300 runs it reproduces the published numbers within sampling error: at scarcity 0.5 the information gap is 0.034 (paper: 0.034), and at scarcity 0.95 the hierarchy collapses in about 86% of runs against about 50% for the star (paper, 30 runs each: 83% and 43%). The full results are in the case study and the paper.
