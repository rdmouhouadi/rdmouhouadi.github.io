About me

For several years I worked in reservoir engineering R&D, with data that was always incomplete and models that were never quite right. You learn quickly to doubt your own assumptions, to check a prediction against what actually happened, and to explain a result to someone who has to act on it.

Those habits turned out to be exactly what machine learning in production demands. Most of the work below started with a method I had never used before, and I stayed with each one until I understood why it worked, or why it didn’t. Today I’m completing an MSc in Data Science & AI at DSTI while working as the data person in the smart metering international department at SUEZ Digital Solutions, and I’m looking for MLOps and ML engineering roles where reliability matters as much as accuracy.

The model was never the hard part. Keeping it right and relevant was.

Selected work

  • MESA

    MESA: when honest AI agents fail together

    An agent-based model of AI agents sharing a limited budget. Their reports degrade quietly, then the whole team collapses.
